# Demon Idol: Iron Legacy — 18-Fighter Clan Roster

## Overview
*My Demon Idol: Iron Legacy* features an 18-fighter roster divided across 6 distinct clans (3 fighters per clan, 12 male, 6 female).
Every fighter is assembled from one of 7 base models, paired with modular hair, signature silhouette accessories, and an expressive anime toon shader with HDR neon emissive highlights for iPhone/URP.

Combat is strictly locked to a 2.5D fighting plane ($Z = 0$) within rich 3D diorama environments.

## Mobile Optimization Budget (iPhone App Store)
- **Triangle Budget**: Max 25,000 tris (LOD0), ~12,500 tris (LOD1).
- **Bone Hierarchy**: Max 75 humanoid bones, 4 weights per vertex.
- **Draw Call Target**: Exactly 1 batched draw call per fighter via single SkinnedMeshRenderer + single texture atlas.
- **Texture Format**: ASTC 6x6 compressed (1024x1024 for base elements, 2048x2048 for final atlas).
- **Shader**: Custom URP Anime Toon (SRP Batcher compatible, zero additional realtime lights required).
