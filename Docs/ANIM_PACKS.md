## 3. Retargeting & Rig Compatibility Certification

- **Humanoid Avatars (24/25 Fighters)**:
  All Humanoid avatars map 1-to-1 with the standard Mecanim bone hierarchy. 
  - Hip, spine, limbs, head, and finger transforms match the normalized T-pose.
  - Foot sliding is prevented by disabling root motion (`applyRootMotion = false`) and handling translation tick-by-tick deterministically in `BBMatchSim.cs`.
- **Generic Rig (1/25 Fighter — EX06 Sentry)**:
  - `EX06 Sentry` utilizes a robotic chassis with generic joint nomenclature.
  - Sentry's attack animations are bound to compatible Spearman/Polearm clips that cleanly drive root, torso, and arm bones without distortion or clipping.
